evidence. New audit access is a shared quota across agents, with three to five
finalists at most under an explicitly declared protocol. Audit errors do not feed
new hypotheses. Full CV requires exactly-once OOF coverage and an explicit fold
mean versus pooled distinction. Evaluating a fixed winner does not evaluate the
entire adaptive search procedure.

verification checks probabilities, class/row order, finite outputs, recursive
recipe/iteration evidence, CSV integrity and input/source posthashes. Technical
completion, quality improvement and audit completeness are separate fields. Full
agent qualification also requires actual stages, Qwen ledger/usage, completed HPO
attempts, final refit and output CSV. Source-only or mocked checks provide none of
these ML claims.
