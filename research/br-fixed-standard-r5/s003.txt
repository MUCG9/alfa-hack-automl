Verified resource sampling — SOURCE ONLY / PREPARED14 / ACTUAL0

Portable dataclass-free psutil + stdlib primitive. No time/clock creation, global
process scan, signal, file/model/API work, heartbeat or delay. The trusted caller
must pass its ORIGINAL non-reentrant clock-only checkpoint; it is checked before
and after every OS observation, including fresh disappearance confirmation.

API:
  VerifiedResourceSampler(process_factory=None)
  .remember(typed_identity_collection)
  .sample(identities, required=[actual_sampler_self], checkpoint=clock_only)
  .confirm_departed(known_identity, checkpoint=clock_only)
  .discover_owned_tree(known_root, checkpoint=clock_only)  # candidate-only use
Known identities are native positive PID + finite positive birth tuples. Process
factory injection is for trusted tests only, with explicit None semantics. A
sample requires at least one mandatory identity; root/caller consumers MUST make
that the actual sampler self. Missing self stops, even if it freshly disappeared.

sample returns schema verified-known-resource-sample-v1: sampled stable RSS rows,
explicit departed rows (rss_bytes NULL), counts and conservative sampled scope.
It freshly opens a PID/birth before and after RSS. It never samples a replacement
PID after detecting reuse. On NoSuchProcess it requires a NEW Process(pid) check:
absence or another birth confirms only the old identity gone. Same original birth
still present, denial, unknown/invalid metadata or a caller checkpoint failure is
sticky. Later absence cannot erase it. Established last_receipt/known provenance
is retained after failure. successful_rounds counts actual COMPLETE RSS rounds;
it is not a clock/heartbeat, process/model/fit counter or full guard PASS.

Caller controls unchanged caps, disk checks, phase/common clocks and .25 successful
sampling cadence/sticky10s gap. Caller advances its last-success timestamp ONLY
after its entire resource sample and checks succeed. It must not call its own RSS
sampling recursively from checkpoint. Sampler keeps no internal clock to reset.

Known-only consumers: C5 observes exact owned identities first, calls sample with
that known set and actual self required. Coordinator NoSuch metadata/RSS consumers
may call confirm_departed; only a returned fresh-confirmed row permits exclusion.
Always retain old known PID/birth for cleanup. Do not treat it as a new ownership
grant or cast a departed RSS to0. AccessDenied is never a benign absence.

Candidate ResourceBudget may discover only one previously supplied owned root's
descendants (never process_iter). New child births must be typed and root stable
around traversal; new/unbound discovery uncertainty is STOP. This is resource
observation, not authority to signal unrelated processes. Strict owned ProcessTree
remains the sole cleanup implementation. Existing memory caps/scopes and conservative
parent double-count are caller policy and are not changed by this primitive.
